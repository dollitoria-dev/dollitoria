# Dollitoria

> Say goodbye to messy Excel spreadsheets. **Dollitoria** is the ultimate, all-in-one companion app designed by a collector, for doll collectors, to catalog, track, and manage your precious collection with absolute ease.

Whether you collect BJD, Blythe, Monster High, Barbie, or rare artist dolls, Dollitoria provides unparalleled flexibility to organize your hobby on the go.

## Key Features

### Advanced Doll Cataloging
* **30+ Specialized Fields:** (brand, mold, skin tone, eye size, height, line, purchase price, country of origin, and more)
* **Photo Attachments:** Upload up to 3 high-quality photos per doll card to create a beautiful visual database.
* **Collection, Wishlist & Archive:** Organize your dolls, keep track of your current collection, manage your dream wishlist, or move dolls to the **Archive** to preserve memories of the ones you sold or parted out.

### Search & Ultimate Flexibility
* **Smart Filtering & Global Search:** Instantly filter your collection by specialized fields, or use keyword search to find a doll in seconds.
* **Custom User Tags:** Assign up to 10 custom tags per doll (e.g., `#needs_faceup`, `#preordered`, `#customized`) for limitless organization and quick tag-based filtering.
* **Visual Gallery:** Browse your entire doll family on a single gallery screen.
* **Recent Cards:** Quickly jump back into your work with a dedicated "Recents" list displaying your last 10 viewed doll cards.

### More Than Just a Database
* **Automated Statistics:** Keep an eye on your hobby's growth with real-time, automated statistical breakdowns of your collection.
* **Artists & Creators Market:** Save contact details and profiles of face-up artists, wigmakers, and outfit creators so you never lose touch with your favorite masters.
* **Dedicated Doll Notes:** A built-in notepad tailored specifically for your hobby thoughts, measurement records, or wish lists.
* **Mini-Games:** Take a break and test your knowledge of doll manufacturers with 2 fun built-in trivia games!

## How to Install the APK
Getting started with open testing is quick and simple:

1. Click here: **[Releases](https://github.com/dollitoria-dev/dollitoria/releases/download/v1.0.0-beta/Dollitoria.apk)** 
2. Open the downloaded file on your Android smartphone.
3. *Note: Android will display a standard warning about installing "apps from unknown sources". This is completely normal for direct APK files. Simply grant permission in your browser/file manager settings to complete the installation.*
